02 Description of service
AOD Comms Analysis is a communication-and-event analysis tool for competitive VALORANT teams. During a session, the Service records each participating player's microphone audio and gameplay screen, retrieves match data from the Riot Games API, and combines both into a synchronized post-match timeline. Coaches and players can review this timeline, along with automatically detected communication patterns, to support coaching and team improvement. All processing happens after a session ends — the Service does not alter gameplay, provide any in-match advantage, or replace official Riot ranking systems.
03 Accounts & teams
You need an account to use the Service, registered as either a Coach or a Player. Coaches may create a team (receiving a shareable team code) or join an existing one; players join a team using a code provided by their coach. The coach who creates a team manages its membership and initiates recorded sessions. Recordings, transcripts, and analysis belong to the team rather than to the coach who created it. If that coach leaves or their account is closed, the team keeps its history and management passes to another coach on the team. If no coach remains, the team is archived — its existing sessions stay readable to its members, but no new sessions can be started until a coach joins.

Recording does not begin until every required participant has acknowledged this notice. You may decline to participate in a session before it starts.

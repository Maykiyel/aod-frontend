Privacy Policy
LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 9, 2026
01 What we collect
The table below maps to what's actually captured by the product, not a generic list — it should stay in sync with the domain model as features change.
DATA SOURCE NOTES
Email, username, role Account signup Used to create your account and identify whether you're registered as a coach or player.
Riot ID, match & game-event data RSO opt-in Only collected after you connect your Riot Account via Riot Sign-On (RSO). Includes match, round, and event data (e.g. kills, deaths, spike plants/defuses) retrieved through the Riot Games API.
Microphone & screen recordings Session recording Only captured after you explicitly acknowledge the in-app recording notice at the start of a session. Captures your voice and your VALORANT window for that session's duration.
Transcripts, callout tags, annotations Generated from recordings Audio is sent to AssemblyAI, our transcription provider, to generate timestamped transcripts. Coaches and players may add written annotations to specific moments on the timeline.
02 How we use it
We use the data above to build a synchronized post-match timeline — aligning your team's voice communication with in-game events pulled from the Riot Games API. This timeline, together with automatically detected communication patterns (frequency, dead air, keyword/callout detection) and any annotations your coach or teammates add, exists solely to support post-match coaching and review. Detection and categorization are automated; we do not generate an automated performance score, rating, or ranking — interpreting what the data means is left to your coaching staff and team, never the system itself.
03 Who we share it with
Session recordings, transcripts, and analysis are visible only to members of your own team (coaches and players who joined using your team's code) — never to opposing teams, other teams on the platform, or the public. We share data with the following third parties, strictly to operate the Service: AssemblyAI (speech-to-text transcription of session audio) and Riot Games (via the Riot Games API/RSO, limited to the match data you've authorized us to retrieve). All recordings, transcripts, and match data are stored locally in a MySQL database for this MVP — no third-party hosting or cloud storage provider is involved. We do not sell your data to advertisers or any other third party, and we do not share it outside your team without your consent.
04 Retention & deletion
Session recordings (audio and screen) are retained for 3 months after a session ends, after which raw audio/video is automatically deleted. Derived timeline data — timestamps, detected communication events, and annotations — may be kept longer to preserve your team's historical analysis, unless you request its removal. You can request deletion of your recordings, transcripts, or your entire account at any time by contacting us at aod-support@gmail.com; we will act on that request within 7–14 business days. If you disconnect your Riot Account, we immediately stop retrieving new match data on your behalf. If our access to the Riot Games API is ever revoked or terminated, we will delete all Game Information obtained through that API, in line with Riot's Developer Terms.
05 Your rights
If you're in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) gives you the right to: be informed about how your data is collected and used; access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; object to certain processing; request erasure or blocking of your data; and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe these rights have been violated. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at aod-support@gmail.com.
06 Security
Recordings, transcripts, and match data are stored locally in a MySQL database for this MVP, and are only accessible to your team's own members and authorized project personnel. Access is controlled at the application level: account passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text, and every request for a recording, transcript, or timeline is checked against your team membership and your role (Coach or Player) before it is served. Session recordings are only ever accessible to members of the team that generated them. This build is a prototype and has not been hardened for production deployment; encryption at rest and transport security will be stated here once the production environment is settled.
07 Children's privacy
You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or take part in a recorded session. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Coaches are responsible for confirming that every player on their roster meets this requirement before starting a recorded session. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, the personal data of minors carries additional protection; rather than handle parental or guardian consent, we have set a strict age floor instead. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18, we will delete that data and close the account.

Questions about this policy: aod-support@gmail.com

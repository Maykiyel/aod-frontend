SCALE Hours of audio, no index A three-hour scrim block is three hours per player. Finding the one late rotate call means scrubbing all of it.
MEMORY Feedback from recall "You went quiet in the retake" is a memory, not a measurement. It gets argued with, and nothing changes.
SILENCE Dead air is invisible Nothing in a VOD shows the six seconds where nobody called the defuse. Absence leaves no trace to review.
THE REVIEW BOARD, AS BUILT OPEN IT →
01 Record the session The coach opens a lobby. Each player grants mic and screen access on their own machine, and both streams open on one session clock when the coach starts. MIC · GAME WINDOW · SYNC OFFSET
02 Classify every call After the session ends, each track is transcribed and every callout is sorted into one of four categories against the team's own keyword sets. Gaps longer than the team's threshold are logged as absence. KEYWORD SETS · DEAD AIR THRESHOLD
03 Review on one board Game events, absence and every player sit as lanes on the same clock. Select a marker to read the call, its matched keywords and the frame it lands on, then leave a note on it. EVENTS · ABSENCE · ONE LANE PER PLAYER
Informative
States what is happening. Enemy positions, counts, utility spent. "ONE DOWN A MAIN"
Declarative
Commits to an action. The speaker is telling the team what they are doing. "TAKING B, FOLLOW ME"
Compound
Information and intent in one breath. The densest call a player can make. "THREE PUSHING, I HAVE ULT"
Absence
An interval longer than the team's threshold with nothing said. Counted, not scored. DEAD AIR 6.4s
Access is granted per session on the player's own machine, before anything is captured. A player who declines stays out of the recording and their interval is counted as absence — the session still runs, and nothing is captured from them.
